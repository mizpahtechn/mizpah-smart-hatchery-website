Cancellation, Refund & Replacement Policy

We aim to handle order changes, cancellations, complaints and refund requests fairly, transparently and professionally.

1. Scope

This policy applies to orders placed with Mizpah Technology for poultry products, day-old chicks and hatchery-related products or services through our website or our official communication channels. Any separate written terms agreed for a particular service or order should also be read with this policy.

2. Requesting a cancellation

  • Customers may request cancellation before an order is accepted for processing.
  • Once processing or dispatch has begun, a cancellation request is subject to Mizpah's review. Approval will depend on the order's status, the work already undertaken, product availability and the circumstances of the request.
  • Please contact Mizpah as soon as possible and provide your order reference and the reason for the request. A cancellation is not confirmed until Mizpah communicates its decision.

3. Refunds and replacements

  • If Mizpah is unable to fulfil an accepted order, the customer may receive a refund or an agreed replacement.
  • The appropriate resolution will be discussed with the customer and confirmed through Mizpah's official communication channels.
  • Refund and replacement requests in other circumstances will be reviewed in light of the order status, product availability and the facts of the case.

4. Live day-old chicks and quality complaints

Day-old chicks are live, time-sensitive products. Customers should inspect their order on receipt and report any shortage, condition or quality concern promptly after delivery or collection so that Mizpah can investigate while the relevant details are available.

Please provide the order reference, a description of the concern, the quantity affected and supporting photographs or video where available. Mizpah will review and verify each report, may request additional information, and will communicate the appropriate resolution.

5. Equipment and hatchery services

Equipment, installation, maintenance, training, consultancy and other services may involve work or preparation specific to the customer's request. Cancellation and refund requests for these items will be assessed according to the order status, work already undertaken, agreed service scope and circumstances. Any specific written terms agreed for a service will also apply.

6. How to submit a request

Contact Mizpah using the official details below. Include your name, order reference, the email address or phone number used for the order, a clear description of the request and any relevant transaction details or supporting evidence.

7. Fair handling of requests

Mizpah will review requests fairly, verify relevant order and transaction information, request further details where necessary, and communicate the outcome to the customer. Customers are encouraged to retain receipts, payment records and correspondence and to cooperate with any reasonable investigation.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or limit any consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable Nigerian law.
