Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information we may collect, why we use it, when it may be shared and the choices available to you when you interact with Mizpah Technology.

1. Who we are

Mizpah Technology (“Mizpah”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates the website mizpahtechnology.com.ng and provides poultry hatchery products, day-old chicks, poultry equipment and related services. Our business address is 24 School Road, Off Itu Road, Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use our website or contact us, we may collect:

  • Contact and identity details such as your name, email address and telephone number.
  • Order and service information such as products requested, quantities, delivery or collection details, order references, enquiries and communications.
  • Payment-related information such as payment reference, amount, status and transaction confirmation. Card details, PINs, passwords and one-time passcodes should be entered only with the relevant payment provider; do not send them to Mizpah.
  • Account information needed to create and maintain a customer account and authenticate access.
  • Basic technical and usage information that may be generated when you visit the website, such as device/browser details, IP address, security events and pages requested.

3. How we use information

We use information where reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, create and manage accounts, process and verify orders and payments, arrange collection or delivery, provide hatchery products and services, communicate order updates, handle complaints and refunds, maintain website security, prevent misuse, keep business records and meet legal obligations.

4. Payments and service providers

Where online payment is offered, payment processing is provided through the payment options presented at checkout, which may include Paystack or OPay. The relevant provider handles payment credentials under its own terms and privacy information. Mizpah receives or uses transaction information needed to confirm and reconcile a payment; we do not ask customers to disclose card PINs, banking passwords or OTPs to us.

We use technology and communications providers to operate parts of the website, database, security and email delivery. These may include website hosting/infrastructure, Supabase, Cloudflare and Resend, as well as payment providers. They may process information on our behalf to provide their services and subject to their applicable terms and safeguards.

5. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share the minimum information reasonably required with service providers supporting our operations, payment providers to complete transactions, delivery or logistics partners when arranging fulfilment, professional advisers where necessary, and public authorities where disclosure is required by law or needed to protect rights, safety or security.

6. Cookies, sessions and website security

The website and account features may use essential browser storage, session technologies or similar mechanisms to support sign-in, security, website functionality and user preferences. Your browser may allow you to control cookies and storage, although disabling essential functions may affect account or order features. We may use technical logs to diagnose errors and protect the website.

7. Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order fulfilment, customer support, accounting, dispute handling, security and legal obligations. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of record and applicable requirements. We use appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Subject to applicable Nigerian data protection law, you may have rights to request access to personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion where applicable, restriction or objection to certain processing, or a copy of information in a portable format where the law provides. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Some information may need to be retained to complete an order or meet legal obligations.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

Our website and services are intended for business customers, poultry farmers and members of the general public able to make or authorise purchases. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children for independent account or purchase activity.

10. External websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or payment services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review those policies before providing information.
