Choosing the right birds

The right choice depends on your production goal, housing, feeding plan, market and experience. Customers can enquire about available broiler, layer, cockerel, Noiler and turkey options. Availability may vary by hatch date and stock; confirm the specific type and quantity with our team before payment.

What to check when receiving chicks

Observe the flock on arrival and promptly raise any concern. Healthy-looking chicks are generally active and alert, with clear eyes, clean bodies, sound legs and no obvious deformities. Follow the supplier's handling guidance and seek veterinary advice if you notice unusual weakness, distress or mortality.

Prepare before arrival

Clean and disinfect the brooding area ahead of time. Pre-heat the brooder, provide clean water and appropriate chick starter feed, prepare suitable litter and ensure adequate ventilation without chilling the birds. Follow a veterinarian-approved vaccination and biosecurity plan for your location.
