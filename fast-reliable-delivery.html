Fast & Reliable Delivery

For live poultry, delivery planning matters. Mizpah works with customers to confirm order details and agree on available delivery or collection arrangements before fulfilment.

Place or enquire about an orderWhy Choose Mizpah

Order confirmation comes first

Confirm the bird type, quantity, current availability, price, intended fulfilment date, destination and contact details. Delivery timing and charges depend on location, route, availability and operating conditions; these details should be agreed before an order is finalised.

Preparing for chick arrival

Have the brooder ready before dispatch or collection. Arrange suitable transport from the receiving point to the farm, prepare clean water and feed, and ensure someone is available to receive and inspect the chicks promptly.

Handling and transport

Live chicks require appropriate ventilation, temperature management, space and careful handling. Avoid unnecessary delays, rough handling and exposure to extreme heat or cold. Transport arrangements should account for journey duration and animal welfare.

Communication and updates

Keep your phone reachable and share accurate directions and an alternative contact where appropriate. If plans change, contact Mizpah as early as possible so the team can review available options.

On receipt

Check the consignment against the confirmed order and promptly report discrepancies or concerns through the agreed support channel. Keep relevant order details and photographs where useful.

How can we help?

Tell us what you need and our team can help clarify availability, requirements, timing and the appropriate next step.
