Incubation equipment and systems

Incubators and hatchers support controlled conditions during the development and hatching process. Equipment should be selected according to capacity, bird species, power availability, operating environment, service support and the operator's production plan.

Egg selection and preparation

Use clean, suitable hatching eggs from healthy breeder flocks. Handle eggs gently, avoid unnecessary washing or temperature shocks, and store them under conditions appropriate to the species and storage duration. Follow equipment and veterinary guidance for sanitation.

Temperature, humidity and ventilation

Temperature, relative humidity, ventilation and egg turning must be managed in line with the incubator manufacturer's instructions and species-specific recommendations. Calibrated instruments, routine checks and accurate records help operators identify deviations early.

Candling, hatch management and hygiene

Candling can help assess embryo development at appropriate stages. Maintain clean equipment, controlled movement of people and materials, and documented cleaning routines between batches. Investigate poor hatch results systematically rather than changing several settings at once.

Equipment selection and support

Before purchasing or installing equipment, confirm capacity, power requirements, backup power arrangements, installation needs, warranty terms, spare-parts availability and operator training. Contact Mizpah to discuss suitable equipment and service requirements.
