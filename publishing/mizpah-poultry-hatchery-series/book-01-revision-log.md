## Changes made

1. Expanded the purpose and scope to identify commercial chicken parent stock as the principal worked model while distinguishing other poultry species and production systems.
2. Rebuilt the contents page to list all numbered chapters in their actual order.
3. Expanded the reproductive biology discussion: ovulation, fertilisation, sperm transfer, sperm-storage tubules, sperm function, and the distinction between infertility and embryo mortality.
4. Added scientific references on poultry reproductive management, sustained fertility in hens, sperm fitness assessment, avian sperm-storage variation and reproductive ageing in male broiler breeders.
5. Expanded breeding-programme design: define the offspring product, plan flock size from realistic egg requirements, and confirm the downstream hatchery and market before placing stock.
6. Expanded male and female reproductive management, including structured fertility investigation, reproductive ageing and the limits of transferring artificial-insemination protocols between species.
7. Added feed-quality assurance, mycotoxin-risk awareness, tropical heat-load management and the need for competent ventilation design.
8. Expanded biosecurity movement controls and farm-to-hatchery loss reconciliation.
9. Added a balanced performance dashboard and clarified the distinction between a sample-based fertility estimate and a whole-batch count.
10. Added a species and production-system comparison chapter covering commercial broiler chickens, layer chickens, indigenous chickens, turkeys, common ducks, Muscovy ducks, geese, guinea fowl and quail.
11. Removed duplicate preliminary references and an obsolete review checklist; removed the duplicate opening subsections in Chapter 13.
12. Retained the restriction against universal sex ratios, feed formulas, vaccination schedules, storage limits or species-independent operating targets.
