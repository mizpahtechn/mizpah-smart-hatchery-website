Written for an international readership

The series will use internationally recognised technical terminology while explaining regional and equipment-related differences instead of assuming that one set of operating conditions suits every hatchery.

Species-aware guidance

Chicken, turkey, duck, Muscovy duck, goose, guinea fowl and quail are covered where the subject requires it. Incubation periods and settings will not be treated as interchangeable.

Practical measurement

Metric units will be primary, with useful Fahrenheit conversions and clear notes about measurement method, incubator type and sensor placement.

Evidence and application

Technical guidance will be checked against poultry-science research, university extension resources, breeder guidance and equipment documentation.

How each book will be prepared

1. Research and technical scope

Define the reader, learning outcomes, species and operational boundaries; check primary and authoritative sources.

2. Original manuscript

Develop clear explanations, practical examples, checklists, records, troubleshooting workflows and case studies.

3. Technical and editorial review

Check calculations, units, references, safety cautions, consistency, originality and readability.

4. Publication and discoverability

Prepare the final e-book, approved cover, metadata, accessible product page, search snippets and related links.

Learn through useful free guidance

The series will be supported by free educational articles and practical short-form lessons. These will answer genuine poultry questions and link readers to relevant books when a deeper guide is available.
