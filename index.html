Frequently Asked Questions

Which day-old chicks do you offer?

Our product categories include broiler DOCs, layer DOCs, and cockerel DOCs. Contact us to confirm availability, quantities, and pricing.

Ask us about custom egg incubation and hatching services, including quantity, timelines, and fees.

How do I get current prices?

Use the enquiry form to request current prices. Availability and pricing should be confirmed before placing an order.
