Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order day-old chicks?

Contact us with the chick type, quantity, preferred date and delivery or collection location. We will confirm availability, pricing and next steps.

Are prices and stock guaranteed on this website?

Prices and availability must be confirmed by Mizpah Technology before payment. Do not treat an enquiry as a confirmed order.

Can I request custom egg incubation?

Yes. Send the egg quantity, egg type and preferred timeline so the team can advise on suitability, pricing and scheduling.

How do I arrange delivery?

Provide your location and preferred date when enquiring. Delivery options, timing and any charges will be confirmed before the order is accepted.

How do I get help after purchase?

Contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp with your order details so we can direct your request to the appropriate team.
